# ✍️ Ultimate Writer Planner

> A complete, free home base for writers — plan books, track word counts, build characters and worlds, manage research, and run your publishing pipeline. All in one Notion space.

---

## 🚀 Start Here

1. Read **❓ How to Use This Template** below.
2. Open **📊 Writing Dashboard** to see your week at a glance.
3. Add your first book in **📚 Book Projects**.
4. Set a target in **🎯 Writing Goals** and start logging in **📈 Word Count Log**.

---

## 🧭 Your Workspace

Everything below imports as a sub-page. Click in to explore.

### Plan
- **📊 Writing Dashboard** — daily command center
- **📚 Book Projects** — every manuscript, status, and word goal
- **📖 Chapters & Scenes** — outline and draft scene by scene
- **🎯 Writing Goals** — quarterly and project targets
- **📅 Writing Schedule** — when and what you'll write

### Create
- **👤 Characters** — full character profiles and arcs
- **🌍 World Building** — settings, lore, magic systems, rules
- **🔬 Research & Notes** — sources, facts, and reference material
- **💡 Idea Inbox** — capture every spark before it's gone

### Track & Ship
- **📈 Word Count Log** — daily writing sessions and streaks
- **📮 Submission Tracker** — queries, agents, contests, publishers
- **✅ Publishing Checklist** — from final draft to launch day

### Reference
- **📐 Story Structure Guide** — beat sheets and frameworks
- **✍️ Daily Writing Ritual** — beat the blank page
- **❓ How to Use This Template** — setup and tips
